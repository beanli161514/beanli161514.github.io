About the method

Read the Cheese3D paper ↗

A segmentation network extracts ear-rim contours from six synchronized cameras. For each ear, a statistical shape model learned across mice represents the rim with 75 ordered 3D points and 15 principal components. The mean point μj is deformed along modes pkj, where αk controls deformation in units of each mode’s standard deviation σk. Rotation R, scale s, and translation t position the ear independently of its shape.

01 / Shape + poseXj=sR(μj+∑k=115αkσkpkj)+t
02 / Camera projectionqij=πi(Xj;Ki,Ri,ti,di)

Each calibrated projection πi includes camera intrinsics Ki, camera pose (Ri, ti), and lens distortion di. Observed contour points are matched to their nearest projected curve samples. Joint fitting minimizes Eimage, combining standardized position, tangent, and curvature errors with per-camera averaging and Huber robustness. Quadratic spacing, physical-length, and coefficient priors constrain incomplete or occluded observations. Frames are fitted individually; previous estimates initialize the next fit without temporal smoothing. In this demo, missing face landmarks are interpolated between valid 3D observations.
