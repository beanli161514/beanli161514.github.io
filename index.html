ABOUT THE METHOD

From images to
deformable 3D curves.

Learning-based segmentation extracts ear contours from six synchronized cameras. Geometric optimization fits a shared 3D curve to those observations, accounting for camera calibration and lens distortion.

A statistical shape model learned across mice describes the ear with 15 principal components, while pose and scale are estimated separately. Face landmarks provide spatial context; the display shows ear rims and a facial landmark skeleton.
