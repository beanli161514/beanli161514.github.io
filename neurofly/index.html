Scalable Annotation for Connectome Construction

A connectome describes how neurons are connected throughout the brain. At the mesoscale, building this map requires reconstructing individual neurons across brain regions. A single neuron’s thin, branching fibers can span distant regions and total tens of centimetres or more. Traditional manual tracing follows these fibers in volumetric images, placing points along every branch.

NeuroFly automatically traces neuron fragments and turns their assembly into structured decisions.
